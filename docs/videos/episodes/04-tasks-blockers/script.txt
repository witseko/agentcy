# Episode 04: Tasks, blockers and handoffs.
# [mark NAME] is where steps.zsh acts; [pause MS] is silence.

[mark hook]
A worker gets stuck. The team unblocks it, and you only watch.
[pause 500]

[mark problem]
In a long review, one stuck agent can stall everyone. In Agentcy, every
task has an owner and a due time, and a stuck owner can ask for help.
[pause 400]

[mark assign]
The lead assigns two tasks: Timeout writes a failing test, and Fixtures
lists the fake APIs. Each task names its owner and the minutes the lead
expects it to take.
[pause 1500]

[mark tasks]
The Tasks tab shows every open task, its owner and when it is due. A task
that runs late is marked overdue, and the lead is told.
[pause 1000]

[mark blocker]
Back in the chat, Timeout cannot fake a timeout, so it replies to its task
with a blocker. The lead wakes up at once.
[pause 1200]

[mark help]
The lead does not solve it alone. It asks Fixtures, the teammate who knows
the tests.
[pause 1000]

[mark evidence]
Fixtures sends the evidence straight to Timeout: a file and a line number.
[pause 1000]

[mark resume]
Only the owner can clear its own blocker. Timeout replies resume, and its
task is open again.
[pause 1000]

[mark handoff]
Now Fixtures offers its task to Timeout with a handoff, and Timeout accepts
it. The task changes owner, and nobody does the work twice.
[pause 1500]

[mark done]
Timeout finishes with done and its evidence: the failing test.
[pause 800]

[mark recap]
So: tasks with owners and due times, blockers that wake the lead, and
handoffs that move work to the right agent.
[pause 800]
