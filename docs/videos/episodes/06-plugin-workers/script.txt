# Episode 06: Fresh read-only workers through plugins.
# [mark NAME] is where steps.zsh acts; [pause MS] is silence.

[mark hook]
No chat open for a worker? Launch a fresh one from the team panel.
[pause 500]

[mark problem]
Opening another app and pasting a join prompt takes time. A plugin starts a
fresh agent for a worker slot and gives it the invitation by itself.
[pause 400]

[mark plugins]
This project has two plugins in its private plugins file: GLM through Z.ai,
and Gemini. Each names the model it runs.
[pause 800]

[mark launch-first]
The lead has two empty worker slots. I select Fixtures in the team panel
and press L. Agentcy fills in the launch command, and I press Enter.
[pause 1500]

[mark states]
The slot shows the launch state, from queued to running, and the worker
joins on its own. The lead keeps working while it starts.
[pause 1500]

[mark launch-second]
For Timeout I type the command myself: slash launch, the name, and the
plugin.
[pause 3500]

[mark work]
Both fresh workers read the brief and their tasks, and send evidence to
each other.
[pause 1500]

[mark read-only]
A fresh worker is read-only. It can read the project and post in the chat,
but it cannot change your files. The lead or you make the changes.
[pause 800]

[mark recap]
So: register a plugin once, then press L on a slot, or type slash launch,
to add a fresh worker.
[pause 800]
