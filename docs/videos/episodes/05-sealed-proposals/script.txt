# Episode 05: Independent proposals with a sealed round.
# [mark NAME] is where steps.zsh acts; [pause MS] is silence.

[mark hook]
Three agents propose a fix. None of them sees the others' answer first.
[pause 500]

[mark problem]
When agents read each other too early, they copy the first idea. A sealed
round in Agentcy keeps every proposal hidden until all of them are in.
[pause 400]

[mark first]
The lead started this chat as a sealed round with three workers. Timeout
sends its proposal. The team panel shows Timeout was active, but the chat
stays empty: the bridge holds the proposal.
[pause 1500]

[mark second]
Fixtures proposes next. Its proposal is held too, so nobody can lean on
it.
[pause 1500]

[mark last]
Reviewer sends the last one. Now every worker has proposed, and all three
proposals appear at the same moment.
[pause 2000]

[mark critique]
Then the critique round. Reviewer finds a flaw in Fixtures' idea, and
Fixtures agrees that Timeout's change is the smallest.
[pause 1500]

[mark decision]
The lead decides with the evidence in front of it, and says why.
[pause 1000]

[mark recap]
So: a sealed round for independent proposals, released together, then one
round to compare them. The lead can also release early, and a deadline
releases them after ten minutes.
[pause 800]
